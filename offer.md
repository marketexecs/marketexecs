# Market Execs

## Target Client
Local barbershops that want a stronger social media presence and more consistent content.

## Core Offer
Market Execs helps barbershops create professional short-form content and maintain a consistent social media presence.

## Starter Package
### Social Content Package

- 4 professionally edited short-form videos per month using footage provided by the client
- Captions written for each video
- A simple monthly content plan
- One revision per video

## Main Goal
Help the barbershop look more professional online, stay consistent and get more attention from potential customers.

## Sales Promise
We do not promise a specific number of customers, followers or views. We focus on producing consistent, professional content that gives the business a stronger online presence.

## Future Services
- Website design
- Social media management
- Brand content
- Digital marketing
- Creative direction
## Founding Client Offer

* **Price:** $100 for the first month
* **Deliverables:** 4 edited short-form videos, 4 captions, a simple monthly content plan and one revision per video
* **Footage:** Provided by the client
* **Publishing:** Videos are delivered to the client; posting is not included
* **Payment:** Agree on payment terms with the client before work begins
* **Availability:** Limited to one founding client while the workflow is being tested
