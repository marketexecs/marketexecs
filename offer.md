# Market Execs

## Target Client
Local barbershops that want a stronger social media presence and more consistent content.

## Core Offer
Market Execs helps barbershops create professional short-form content and maintain a consistent social media presence.

## Starter Package
### Social Content Package

- 8 short-form videos per month
- Captions for every post
- Content ideas and planning
- Basic posting strategy
- Monthly content review

## Main Goal
Help the barbershop look more professional online, stay consistent and get more attention from potential customers.

## Sales Promise
We do not promise a specific number of customers, followers or views. We focus on producing consistent, professional content that gives the business a stronger online presence.
